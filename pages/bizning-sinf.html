Who we are

We are the 11th grade students of IT Park Junior Developers. Every day we write code, solve problems and work on real projects, guided by our mentor. Here is a short look at our class.

Our team

We are a friendly group that helps each other. When one of us gets stuck on a bug, the whole class jumps in to solve it.

What we learn

HTML, CSS, PHP, MySQL and Python. We learn the basics first, then use them to build complete websites and applications.

Our projects

Landing pages, learning-center systems and school websites. Every project is a chance to practice teamwork and clean code.

Life in our class

Hackathons, debates, sports and tea breaks. We study hard, and we also make sure we enjoy being together.

What we believe in

  • Curiosity We ask questions and test ideas.
  • Teamwork We build better when we build together.
  • Discipline We practice every day, not only before deadlines.
  • Honesty We give and accept feedback openly.
